# Project Charter

## Project
AI-Powered Chatbot Development for Customer Support Automation

## Organisation
AussieCart Pty Ltd (fictional case study)

## Objectives
- Improve customer support availability.
- Automate common customer enquiries.
- Provide escalation to human support agents.
- Improve customer satisfaction.

## Scope
Planning, designing, developing, testing and deploying
a proposed AI-powered customer support chatbot.

## Constraints
Duration: 22 weeks
Budget: AUD 120,000

## Key Deliverables
- Project charter and scope
- WBS and Gantt chart
- Risk register
- Change management plan
- Quality and communication plans
